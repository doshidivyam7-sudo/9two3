# Eicher Motors Limited (EICHERMOT.NS) - First Look

**Verdict:** WATCHLIST &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Conviction:** 3/5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Screen score:** 67.5

> Genuine franchise with debt-free 26% ROCE, but the GST-cut volume surge is fading fast and 33x already pays for it.

## Why it screened
The only non-IT name with real growth. Q1 FY27 revenue was +31.2% YoY (₹6,632 cr reported) and PAT +21.3%. ROE is 23.8%, ROCE 26.4%, and there is effectively no debt. The 3-year revenue CAGR of 17.5% says this isn't a one-quarter fluke. P/E of 33.2x is near the top of the screen's 45x cap.

## What the business does
Royal Enfield (mid-size 250–750cc motorcycles, India plus a growing export book) is essentially all of consolidated revenue. Eicher also owns ~54% of VE Commercial Vehicles, a trucks and buses JV with Volvo, which is equity-accounted (Q1 revenue ₹6,610 cr, +16.6%).

## What looks good
- Record Q1: 332,940 Royal Enfield units (+27%), with launches including the Bullet 650, Hunter 350 refresh and first Flying Flea C6 EV deliveries.
- GST 2.0 (Sept 2025) cut the rate on ≤350cc bikes from 28% to 18%. That is where most Royal Enfield volume sits, and it widened the addressable market in rural India.
- Exports are growing faster than domestic (+17% in Sept 2026).
- Clean balance sheet funds capacity internally (₹1,225 cr Phase I greenfield plant at Tada, AP).

## What could be wrong
- **Growth is decelerating sharply.** Volumes went from +27% in Q1, to +11% in Aug 2026 (126,479), to **+8% in Sept 2026** (133,958). From October the comparison base includes the post-GST-cut surge, so headline growth could go to low single digits.
- **The >350cc mix is shrinking.** From the September split (≤350cc + EV 121,495 of 133,958), bigger bikes were ~12,460 units against ~16,850 a year ago, about **−26%**. That is consistent with those models now sitting in the higher GST slab (40%, verify). The 650s carry the best margins.
- **Margins are not expanding.** EBITDA grew ~32%, in line with revenue, and PAT grew 21% on 31% revenue growth. Launch marketing, EV brand spend and a richer ≤350cc mix weigh on margins.
- EV (Flying Flea) is an early-stage cash drain with uncertain payback.

## Valuation sanity check
33.2x trailing (25.6x forward per Yahoo), 7.6x book, 1.2% yield. Price is ₹6,968, 15.3% below the 52-week high (₹8,230) and flat over 12 months. 33x is fair for a debt-free, mid-20s-ROCE franchise compounding mid-teens. It is rich if volume growth settles in the mid-single digits, which the last two months point to. There is no margin of safety at this price.

## Recent developments
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹6,632 cr (+31.5%), PAT ₹1,463 cr (+21.4%), Tada capex approved.
- Aug 2026 volumes +11%; Sept 2026 +8% (domestic +7%, exports +17%).

## Questions a deep-dive must answer
1. Underlying demand ex-GST pull-forward: what is H2 FY27 volume growth on the higher base?
2. How much of the >350cc decline is GST pricing versus model-cycle timing? What is the margin hit from the mix shift?
3. Why did PAT lag revenue by 10 points (other income, JV share, tax, depreciation)?
4. EV losses and capex trajectory; Tada plant utilisation plan.
5. Export run-rate and the CKD-assembly economics.

Revisit on a pullback toward ~25x trailing, or if H2 volumes hold above 10%.
