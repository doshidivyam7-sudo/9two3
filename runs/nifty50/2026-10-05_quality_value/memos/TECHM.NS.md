# Tech Mahindra Limited (TECHM.NS) - First Look

**Verdict:** PASS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Conviction:** 3/5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Screen score:** 57.5

> A margin turnaround that is working but already priced at 26.6x. Half the screened growth is the rupee, and ROE barely clears 15%.

## Why it screened
Revenue +17.7% and PAT +28.4% in Q1 FY27 look like the fastest growth among the IT hits. In dollars, revenue was $1,660m, **+6.1% YoY (+6.6% constant currency)**. The rest is currency. The PAT jump is margin recovery: EBIT margin is 14.4%, up 330bp YoY. ROE of 16.9% clears the 15% bar by less than two points, and the 3-year revenue CAGR is only 2.2%.

## What the business does
Mahindra-group IT services firm, historically telecom-heavy (network services, the Comviva products arm), with growing BFSI and manufacturing exposure. It is in year three of a turnaround under CEO Mohit Joshi.

## What looks good
- Execution on the plan: three consecutive quarters of >$1bn deal wins (Q1 TCV $1,078m, +33% YoY), and all verticals growing YoY.
- Margin trajectory: 14.4% EBIT, with management targeting ~15% for FY27 and a >15% exit rate.
- Constant-currency growth of 6.6% is the best among the IT hits (TCS 3.2%, HCL 2.6%).
- Debt-free; 3.3% dividend yield.

## What could be wrong
- **The earnings base is just a recovery.** FY26 PAT of ₹4,811 cr is back to roughly FY23's ₹4,831 cr, after the FY24 trough of ₹2,358 cr. Profits have not grown over three years; they have recovered.
- **The easy part is done.** Reaching ~15% margin is now consensus. Upside needs a credible FY28+ path to peer-level margins (17–18%+), which TechM has never sustained.
- ~88% payout in FY26; sustaining both the yield and investment needs continued margin gains.
- Telecom-client concentration and Comviva seasonality add volatility. AI-disruption risk is sector-wide.

## Valuation sanity check
26.6x trailing (17.5x forward per Yahoo) and 4.6x book. That is the richest of the IT hits, with the lowest ROE. The forward multiple only looks reasonable if the FY27 margin target is fully delivered. Price is ₹1,539, 17% below the 52-week high and up 6.5% over 12 months, outperforming peers, so the turnaround is partly in the price already. For Indian IT exposure from this screen, TCS offers better returns at a lower multiple.

## Recent developments
- Q1 FY27 (Jul 2026): revenue ₹15,712 cr, PAT ₹1,465 cr, EBIT 14.4%. The stock gained ~3% post-results; Nomura and Jefferies were constructive on FY27.
- Management reiterated its aspiration to grow above the industry's 3–5% in FY27.

## Questions a deep-dive must answer
(Only if revisited.) 1. What is the FY28 margin roadmap beyond 15%? 2. How much is Comviva and telecom exposed to a capex downturn? 3. What do deal wins look like net of renewals?

Revisit if the stock de-rates toward ~18x trailing while margins keep improving.
