# Hindustan Unilever Limited (HINDUNILVR.NS) - First Look

**Verdict:** PASS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Conviction:** 3/5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Screen score:** 42.5

> The 30.7% ROE is flattered by a ₹4,611 cr demerger gain. Underlying it's a 2%-a-year grower at 41x after one good quarter.

## Why it screened
ROE of 30.7%, no debt, and 10.3% revenue growth in Q1 FY27. The ROE is **inflated**. FY26 net income of ₹15,040 cr includes a **₹4,611 cr non-cash exceptional gain** on the Kwality Wall's ice-cream demerger, booked in Q3 FY26. Excluding it, net income is ~₹10,430 cr, which puts ROE at roughly **21%**: still above the 15% bar, but a third lower. Earnings growth reads −3.0% because the base quarter had a one-off tax credit; excluding exceptionals, PAT grew 9%.

## What the business does
India's largest FMCG company (Unilever ~62%): home care, beauty and personal care, and foods. Ice cream was demerged into Kwality Wall's (India) in December 2025.

## What looks good
- Q1 FY27 turnover of ₹17,184 cr (+10%) was the best growth in 13 quarters, with underlying volume growth of 5% and price/mix contributing the rest.
- EBITDA margin held at 23.0%, within management's band; EBITDA grew 8%.
- Strong cash conversion; ~92% payout; debt-free.
- A new CEO (Priya Nair) and a simplified portfolio give the turnaround a real chance.

## What could be wrong
- **One quarter does not make a trend.** The 3-year revenue CAGR is 2.3%, and FY26 core PAT growth was ~1% in Q3. Rural recovery and GST-led consumption support may not persist.
- **Like-for-like growth is unclear.** Ice cream left the business mid-FY26. Is +10% measured on a restated continuing-operations base?
- Margins lagged sales: EBITDA +8% on sales +10%. The stock fell ~6% on the print.
- Competition from D2C brands and quick-commerce private labels in beauty and personal care keeps pricing power in check.
- **The price history is distorted.** The −31% from the 52-week high (₹2,667) and −27.8% over 12 months partly reflect the December 2025 demerger, when value moved into Kwality Wall's shares. The stock is not as cheap as the chart suggests.

## Valuation sanity check
41.1x trailing (34.5x forward per Yahoo), 8.8x book, 2.4% yield. For ~2–5% medium-term volume growth and a ~21% underlying ROE, that is full value. It would take a sustained high-single-digit growth recovery to justify it, and that is already the bull case. Price is ₹1,831, near the 52-week low of ₹1,827, but see the demerger caveat above.

## Recent developments
- Q1 FY27 (28 Jul 2026): turnover +10%, volume growth 5%, EBITDA margin 23.0%, PAT before exceptionals ₹2,731 cr (+9%).
- Q3 FY26: ₹4,611 cr demerger gain; core PAT +1%.

## Questions a deep-dive must answer
(Only if revisited.) 1. What is like-for-like growth ex-ice cream? 2. Can volume growth hold at 5% or better for four quarters? 3. What are the margin levers under the new CEO?

Revisit after two more quarters of 5%+ volume growth, or at sub-30x trailing.
