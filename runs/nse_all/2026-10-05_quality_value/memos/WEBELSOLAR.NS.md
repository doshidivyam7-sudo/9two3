# Websol Energy System Limited (WEBELSOLAR.NS) - First Look

**Verdict:** PASS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Conviction:** 3/5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Screen score:** 81.3

> Sub-scale solar-cell maker with margins already compressing into a module glut. Promoters own 30%, and until recently 80% of that was pledged.

## Why it screened
ROE 66.7%, P/E 9.5, revenue +70.3%. The 3-year revenue CAGR of 293% is off a near-zero base: FY24 revenue was ₹26 cr while the plant was being rebuilt. More telling, **growth is decelerating into the cost line**. Q1 FY27 revenue rose 70%, but PAT only 16%, and EBITDA margin fell from 47% to 34% as the mix shifted to lower-margin modules.

## What the business does
Kolkata-based solar cell and module maker; ~1 GW/yr of cell output (259 MW in Q1 at 92% utilisation).

## What looks good
- The IREDA loan has been prepaid, and the promoter pledge fell from 80% to 16%.
- Cells are the scarce link under the ALMM-II domestic-cell mandate (from June 2026).

## What could be wrong
- **Industry glut:** India has 233 GW of module capacity against ~50 GW of annual installations, with another 135 GW in the pipeline. Module pricing is under pressure, and Websol is sub-scale against integrated majors.
- Promoter holding is only 29.7%, and the pledge history is a governance flag.
- The stock is 50.7% off its 52-week high and down 41.7% over 12 months. It locked a 5% lower circuit despite 70% revenue growth.

## Valuation sanity check
9.5x trailing looks cheap, but earnings are at risk from margin compression. If EBITDA margins normalise to the low 20s, earnings would fall.

## Recent developments
- Q1 FY27 (Aug 2026): revenue ₹373 cr, PAT ₹78 cr; IREDA loan repaid.
