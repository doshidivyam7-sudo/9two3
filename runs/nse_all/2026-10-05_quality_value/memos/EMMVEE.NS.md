# Emmvee Photovoltaic Power Limited (EMMVEE.NS) - First Look

**Verdict:** DEEP-DIVE &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Conviction:** 2/5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Screen score:** 83.7

> An integrated solar module-and-cell maker. Cells are the bottleneck under ALMM-II, and Emmvee is tripling cell capacity. But margins are at a record high in a module glut.

## Why it screened
ROE 51.1%, ROCE 34.5%, D/E 0.1 and P/E 15.9, with revenue +51.3% and earnings +102.6%. Unlike most of the whole-market top 10, **these are recurring operating earnings**, not a one-off. Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,555.5 cr, EBITDA ₹548 cr (a record 35.2% margin) and PAT ₹380 cr. The 102.7% 3-year revenue CAGR is real scale-up: revenue went from ₹942 cr in FY24 to ₹5,034 cr in FY26.

## What the business does
Bengaluru-based maker of solar PV modules (10.3 GW installed) and TOPCon cells (2.94 GW), selling to IPPs, EPCs and rooftop channels. Promoters own ~80%.

## What looks good
- **The cell position is the thesis.** ALMM List II, in force since June 2026, requires domestically made cells for covered projects. India has ~233 GW of module capacity but projects only ~29 GW of cell output next year against ~50 GW of demand. Integrated players with cells capture that scarcity. Cell utilisation is already 83%.
- Visibility: a record 9.9 GW order book (1.48 GW added in Q1).
- Expansion to ~16.3 GW of modules and ~8.9 GW of cells by early FY28 (module line due Dec 2026, cell line Mar 2027).
- Demand backdrop: India is on track for a record ~50 GW of installations in 2026.

## What could be wrong
- **Peak margins.** A 35% EBITDA margin in module manufacturing is far above industry norms (~25% in FY25, expected to moderate). Even Emmvee's own module lines run at only 45% utilisation, which is the glut showing up in its own numbers.
- **Capex and execution:** tripling cell capacity in ~9 months is a big bet. If cell scarcity eases by FY28–29 as more capacity arrives, the new lines come on just as pricing normalises.
- Upstream dependence: wafers and polysilicon are likely imported (verify). Trade or policy changes could squeeze margins.
- Policy-driven demand (ALMM, PLI, duties) can be withdrawn or diluted.

## Valuation sanity check
At 15.9x trailing on record margins, the market already discounts normalisation. If EBITDA margins fall to ~22–25%, earnings would drop ~30–35% from current levels, all else equal, putting the stock in the low 20s on a P/E basis. That is fair for a grower, not obviously cheap. Upside requires the cell capacity to be filled at margins well above module-only peers. Price is ₹297, 20% below the 52-week high and up 39% over 12 months.

## Recent developments
- Q1 FY27 (Jul 2026): PAT +103%, module output 970 MW (+53%), cell output 454 MW (+26%).

## Questions a deep-dive must answer
1. What are cell-level versus module-level margins? How much of the 35% is cell scarcity rent?
2. Cost and funding of the cell expansion; payback at normalised pricing.
3. Wafer sourcing: China dependence and the cost of duty changes.
4. Order book quality: customer mix, pricing terms, cancellation clauses.
5. What does Emmvee look like if ALMM-II enforcement slips?

Conviction is 2 because the bull case depends on margins staying well above history.
