# Chennai Petroleum Corporation Limited (CHENNPETRO.NS) - First Look

**Verdict:** PASS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Conviction:** 4/5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Screen score:** 80.3

> A refiner at a margin peak. GRMs went from $3.2 to $8.8/bbl, and a 4.9x P/E on peak earnings is the usual cyclical trap.

## Why it screened
P/E 4.9, ROE 32.1%, revenue growth 84.8%, 4.6% dividend yield. The swing is the refining cycle. The Q1 FY27 GRM was **$8.78/bbl against $3.22** a year earlier, turning a ₹40 cr loss into ₹1,031 cr of profit. Revenue also includes ₹385 cr from a retrospective product-price revision. The earnings record is volatile: FY23 PAT ₹3,532 cr, FY25 ₹214 cr, FY26 ₹3,103 cr. The 3-year revenue CAGR is −6%.

## What the business does
Indian Oil Corporation subsidiary running refineries at Manali (~10.5 MMTPA) and Nagapattinam (with a new 9 MMTPA project under way with IOC).

## What looks good
- Strong current cash generation and high utilisation (management projects up to 108.6%), with flexible crude sourcing (Russia, West Africa).
- 1.8x book; the Nagapattinam expansion adds volume later in the decade.

## What could be wrong
- GRMs mean-revert. FY25 shows what a normal year can look like.
- Policy risk: as a PSU, retail price controls and windfall taxes can cap upside.
- The Nagapattinam capex could re-lever the balance sheet (D/E is 0.2 today).

## Valuation sanity check
Price-to-book (1.8x) and through-cycle earnings are the right lens, not a peak P/E. Averaging FY23–FY26 PAT (~₹2,400 cr, including the FY25 trough) gives ~8.4x: reasonable, but not the 4.9x headline. In a FY25-type year the multiple would be ~95x.

## Recent developments
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹27,369 cr (+85%), PAT ₹1,031 cr; the stock fell ~3% on the print as margins declined sequentially.

## Questions a deep-dive must answer
Not a fit for a compounder mandate.
