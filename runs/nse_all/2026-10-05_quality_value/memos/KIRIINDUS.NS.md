# Kiri Industries Limited (KIRIINDUS.NS) - First Look

**Verdict:** PASS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Conviction:** 4/5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Screen score:** 96.7

> The 0.6x P/E is a one-off ₹5,881 cr DyStar award. The operating dye business loses money, and the cash is going into copper.

## Why it screened
Top score in the whole market (96.7) on a P/E of 0.6, ROE of 115% and earnings growth of 2,764%. **All of it is one exceptional item.** FY26 net income of ₹5,567 cr includes ₹5,881 cr of exceptional income from monetising the DyStar stake ($689m awarded through the Singapore International Commercial Court). Strip that out and the business loses money: operating income was negative in each of FY23–FY26 (FY26 −₹84 cr on ₹822 cr revenue). The 3-year revenue CAGR is −3.6%.

## What the business does
Dyes, dye intermediates and basic chemicals (Gujarat). Until 2026 its main value was a 37.6% stake in DyStar (Singapore), which was tied up in a decade of litigation with Zhejiang Longsheng/Senda.

## What looks good
- The cash is real. About ₹5,854 cr was received, standalone debt has been repaid, and the market cap (₹3,548 cr, 0.5x book) is below the proceeds.
- The litigation overhang has been resolved.

## What could be wrong
- **Capital allocation.** The company is pivoting into copper, a new, capital-intensive business with no track record. This is the classic way a cash windfall gets destroyed.
- The core business is structurally loss-making at the operating line.
- Tax, receiver costs and Senda's residual claim may leave less net cash than the headline.
- The mandate avoids governance risk. A long litigation history and a pivot away from the core warrant caution.

## Valuation sanity check
This is a sum-of-parts / net-cash question, not a P/E question. Trailing multiples are meaningless. If net cash really exceeds the market cap, it may be a special situation, but it belongs to a different mandate from a quality compounder screen.

## Recent developments
- FY26: consolidated PAT of ₹5,379 cr on the DyStar exceptional gain; standalone debt-free.
- May 2026: copper pivot announced alongside the settlement.

## Questions a deep-dive must answer
(Only for a special-situations mandate.) What is net cash after tax and Senda? What is the copper capex plan and its expected returns? Is a buyback or special dividend possible?
