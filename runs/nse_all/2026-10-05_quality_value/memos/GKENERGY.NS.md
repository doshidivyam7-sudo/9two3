# GK Energy Limited (GKENERGY.NS) - First Look

**Verdict:** WATCHLIST &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Conviction:** 2/5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Screen score:** 82.0

> Fast-growing, cheap solar-pump EPC. But it depends on one government subsidy scheme, and the receivables are unverified.

## Why it screened
ROE 37.3%, ROCE 34.6%, P/E 9.9 and D/E 0.2, with revenue +55.5% and earnings +59.9%. The 3-year revenue CAGR of 81.8% is real scale-up: revenue went from ₹411 cr in FY24 to ₹1,714 cr in FY26, with PAT up from ₹36 cr to ₹204 cr. Q1 FY27 revenue was ~₹500 cr with PAT of ~₹60 cr. Market cap of ₹2,310 cr is just above the screen's ₹2,000 cr floor.

## What the business does
Pune-based EPC provider of solar-powered agricultural pumps under Component B of **PM-KUSUM**. It is empanelled in Maharashtra, Haryana, Rajasthan, UP and MP, which together hold ~82% of sanctioned pump volume. It describes its model as asset-light. Promoters own ~82%.

## What looks good
- Leadership in the largest PM-KUSUM states, plus new state orders (e.g. MSEDCL: 10,000 off-grid pumps, ₹236 cr).
- Operating margin ~16% and a high ROE for an EPC business.
- The valuation (9.9x) is low, and the stock is 52.5% below its 52-week high.

## What could be wrong
- **Scheme concentration:** nearly all revenue depends on PM-KUSUM sanctions and the central and state subsidy flow. Any change to the scheme's timelines or funding would hit revenue directly.
- **Receivables and working capital:** the customers are state agencies, which are often slow payers. I could not verify receivable days, and this is the key number.
- An EPC business at this growth rate typically consumes cash; check operating cash flow against PAT.
- Recently listed and small, with limited track record or disclosure history.

## Valuation sanity check
9.9x on fast-growing earnings looks cheap. A low multiple on a scheme-dependent EPC with uncertain cash conversion is common, and often deserved. If cash flow tracks profit, it is attractive. If receivables are ballooning, it is a trap.

## Recent developments
- Q1 FY27 (Aug 2026): revenue +56%, PAT +60%; the stock rose ~10% on results but is down ~34% over 12 months.

## Questions a deep-dive must answer
1. Receivable days and the ageing of state-agency dues; OCF/PAT over FY25–FY27.
2. PM-KUSUM's status and funding beyond the current phase; the pipeline in each state.
3. Competitive intensity and pricing in state tenders.

Move to DEEP-DIVE only if cash conversion checks out.
