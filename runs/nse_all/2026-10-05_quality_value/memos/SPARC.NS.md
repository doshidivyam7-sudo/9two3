# Sun Pharma Advanced Research Company Limited (SPARC.NS) - First Look

**Verdict:** PASS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Conviction:** 5/5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Screen score:** 81.8

> The 277% ROE and 3.9x P/E come from selling one FDA priority review voucher for $195m. The R&D business burns cash.

## Why it screened
ROE 276.9%, P/E 3.9, revenue growth 314%. All of it is one transaction. Q4 FY26 revenue of ₹1,853 cr (against ₹8 cr the quarter before) came from the **sale of a USFDA Priority Review Voucher** for $195m, granted for Sezaby. Excluding it, SPARC books ~₹8–40 cr of quarterly revenue and **loses ₹50–80 cr a quarter**; Q1 FY27 was back to a ₹21 cr loss. The operating margin reads −90.7%, and the 3-year revenue CAGR is −45%.

## What the business does
Pure-play pharma R&D, spun out of Sun Pharma (promoter group ~73%). It runs novel drug-delivery and NCE programmes that it licenses out or sells.

## What looks good
- The PRV cash extends the runway for several years of R&D spend.
- Optionality on pipeline assets.

## What could be wrong
- There are no recurring earnings. Another PRV or licensing deal is not forecastable.
- Its history includes repeated clinical setbacks and losses every year from FY23 to FY25.

## Valuation sanity check
Not valuable on earnings. Value is cash plus a probability-weighted pipeline, a biotech-style analysis outside this screen's scope.

## Recent developments
- Feb 2026: PRV granted; sold 30 Apr 2026 for $195m. FY26 standalone PAT ₹1,552 cr against a ₹345 cr loss in FY25.
