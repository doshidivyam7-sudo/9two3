# National Aluminium Company Limited (NATIONALUM.NS) - First Look

**Verdict:** WATCHLIST &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Conviction:** 2/5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Screen score:** 86.4

> A low-cost, debt-free aluminium/alumina PSU with real volume growth coming. But 9.2x is a peak-cycle multiple on 48% operating margins.

## Why it screened
P/E 9.2, ROE 29.4%, ROCE 33.3%, zero debt, revenue +39.3% and earnings +90.9%. These are real earnings, but **cyclical**. The operating margin is 47.6% today against ~16% in FY24 (operating income ₹2,134 cr on ₹13,070 cr revenue), when PAT was ₹1,988 cr against ₹5,797 cr in FY26. Q1 FY27 PAT was ₹2,002 cr (+88%) at a 51% EBITDA margin. The 3-year revenue CAGR is 7.8%.

## What the business does
Government-controlled (~51%) integrated producer: bauxite mines and alumina refinery at Damanjodi, an aluminium smelter at Angul, and captive power. It is a significant alumina exporter.

## What looks good
- **Volume growth:** the 1 MTPA fifth-stream alumina refinery is due to start commercial production in Nov–Dec 2026, reaching full capacity in FY28 and lifting alumina capacity to 3.2 MTPA.
- Realisations are turning up: alumina at $323/t in Q1, with management guiding ~$370/t for Q2.
- Integrated bauxite gives a cost advantage; the balance sheet is debt-free.

## What could be wrong
- **Costs are rising:** aluminium cost of production climbed to ~₹1.70 lakh/t from ~₹1.57 lakh/t (caustic soda, coal, fuel oil).
- Alumina prices are volatile. The new refinery's volumes arrive into whatever the price is in 2027–28.
- PSU capital allocation, government dividend demands and a possible OFS overhang.
- The stock is up 54% over 12 months, so much of the upcycle is priced.

## Valuation sanity check
At 9.2x peak earnings and 2.9x book, the stock is fair-to-full for a cyclical. On FY24-type earnings plus the new refinery's volumes, the normalised P/E would be in the high teens. The time to buy is when trailing P/E is high at the bottom of the cycle, not now.

## Recent developments
- Q1 FY27 (31 Jul 2026): revenue ₹5,302 cr (+39%), EBITDA ₹2,708 cr (+81%); targeting 1.6 MT of alumina sales in FY27.

## Questions a deep-dive must answer
1. What are through-cycle EBITDA per tonne and the refinery's IRR at mid-cycle alumina prices?
2. Smelter expansion plans and power cost.
3. Dividend policy under government ownership.

Revisit in a down-cycle, or if the refinery ramp beats plan.
