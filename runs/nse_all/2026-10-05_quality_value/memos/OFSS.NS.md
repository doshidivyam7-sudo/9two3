# Oracle Financial Services Software Limited (OFSS.NS) - First Look

**Verdict:** WATCHLIST &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Conviction:** 3/5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Screen score:** 81.6

> High-quality, cash-returning banking-software franchise. But Q1's surge is a ₹935 cr one-off licence, and it could mean losing that client's recurring work.

## Why it screened
ROE 49.9%, ROCE 43.2%, zero debt, 59.6% operating margin and a 3.8% yield, with revenue +68.7% and earnings +120.5%. Growth is the distorted part. Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹3,125 cr included **₹935 cr of licence revenue from a single agreement with a global bank**. Excluding it, revenue is ~₹2,190 cr, about +18% YoY: good, but nowhere near +69%. The 3-year revenue CAGR is 10.4%.

## What the business does
Oracle Corp's ~72%-owned listed subsidiary. It sells Flexcube core banking, analytics and payments software to banks globally, plus a small consulting arm.

## What looks good
- Durable franchise: mission-critical core-banking software, ~60% operating margins, no debt.
- Distributes nearly all its earnings, hence the 3.8% yield.
- Underlying growth (~18% ex the deal) is healthy for a mature product company.

## What could be wrong
- **The nature of the deal.** It covers licensing, a *transfer of personnel* and transition services with an existing customer. That reads like a bank buying out the platform and taking it in-house. If so, Q1's windfall could be followed by lost recurring support and services revenue from that client.
- **Leadership change:** long-time CEO Makarand Padalkar resigned, and CFO Avadhut Ketkar takes over.
- Minority shareholders depend on Oracle Corp's priorities (product roadmap, cloud migration, transfer pricing). There have been past delisting rumours.
- Bank software deals are lumpy, so quarterly growth will swing.

## Valuation sanity check
26.7x trailing includes the one-off. If most of the ₹935 cr flows to PAT, underlying trailing earnings are ~₹2,700 cr and the multiple is **~33–35x**, with a 13x P/B. That is a full price for ~10–18% growth, even with a high-quality yield. The stock reversed an early 5% gain after results. Price is ₹10,430, 16.8% below the 52-week high.

## Recent developments
- Q1 FY27 (Jul 2026): revenue +69%, PAT ₹1,416 cr (+121%), EBITDA margin 60%; CEO succession announced.

## Questions a deep-dive must answer
1. Deal specifics: does the client stop paying recurring support or services fees?
2. Underlying licence pipeline and SaaS/cloud transition economics.
3. The new CEO's priorities and Oracle's stance on the listed subsidiary.

Revisit on a pullback toward ~25x underlying earnings.
