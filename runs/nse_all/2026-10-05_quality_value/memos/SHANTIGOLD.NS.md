# Shanti Gold International Limited (SHANTIGOLD.NS) - First Look

**Verdict:** PASS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Conviction:** 3/5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Screen score:** 80.0

> Gold-price pass-through dressed as growth. It's a 6%-net-margin jewellery job shop with one dominant client, near its 52-week high.

## Why it screened
Revenue +144.7% and a 3-year CAGR of 43.8%, with ROE 38.1% and P/E 11.1. Much of the top-line growth is **the gold price**: Indian gold went from ~₹1 lakh to above ₹1.5 lakh per 10 g in about a year, and a manufacturer's revenue moves with it. Profit grew far less: Q1 FY27 PAT was +46.9% YoY and *down* 2.8% QoQ. The operating margin is 9.9% and the net margin 6.4%.

## What the business does
Mumbai manufacturer of 22kt CZ-casting gold jewellery for retail chains. It listed in August 2025 (IPO at ₹199, 81x subscribed).

## What looks good
- Capacity is growing: the Marol facility added ~4,000 kg/yr from June 2026.
- The stock is near its 52-week high (₹333), so the market is buying the growth.

## What could be wrong
- **Client concentration:** Lalithaa Jewellery Mart is described as a core client, and Shanti has just bought ₹3.88 cr of Lalithaa shares. Investing in a key customer is an odd use of capital and a related-party-style flag.
- Working capital and gold-inventory financing (D/E 0.4) scale with gold prices. A price fall hits both revenue and inventory value.
- Demand is shifting to lightweight jewellery at record gold prices.

## Valuation sanity check
11.1x on earnings that ride the gold price. On a thin-margin, inventory-heavy model, there is no quality premium to justify.

## Recent developments
- Q1 FY27: total income ₹718 cr, PAT ₹50.5 cr.

## Questions a deep-dive must answer
Not recommended for this mandate.
