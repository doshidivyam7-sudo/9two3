# Hindustan Zinc Limited (HINDZINC.NS) - First Look

**Verdict:** PASS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Conviction:** 4/5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Screen score:** 82.7

> A world-class low-cost miner riding a silver boom at peak margins. It is Vedanta-controlled, and its ROE is inflated by paying out its equity.

## Why it screened
ROE 76.9%, ROCE 62%, revenue +71.7%, earnings +144.8%, P/E 13.9. It is an excellent asset, but the numbers reflect **commodity prices**. Q1 FY27 PAT of ₹5,469 cr (+145%) was driven by silver: silver revenue rose 169% to ₹3,839 cr. The ROE is high partly because years of special dividends have shrunk equity (P/B 10.5). The 3-year revenue CAGR is only 5.6%; FY24 PAT was ₹7,759 cr, against ₹13,832 cr in FY26.

## What the business does
India's dominant integrated zinc-lead-silver producer (Rampura Agucha and other Rajasthan mines). Vedanta owns ~62% (insiders ~89% including the government's stake).

## What looks good
- Bottom-quartile cost: zinc cost of production fell 16% to $851/t, and Q1 mined metal was a record 268 kt.
- Silver gives a natural hedge. D/E is down to 0.31 from 1.19.

## What could be wrong
- **The cycle.** Silver and zinc prices drive nearly all the earnings change.
- **Governance:** the controlling shareholder Vedanta is highly levered and has historically drawn cash up via special dividends and brand fees. The mandate avoids governance risk.
- A government OFS overhang could weigh on the stock.

## Valuation sanity check
13.9x trailing at cycle-high silver and 10.5x book. On FY24-type earnings the stock would be ~30x. This is a commodity-price call, not a quality-at-reasonable-price one.

## Recent developments
- Q1 FY27 (Jul 2026): record revenue (₹13,747 cr), EBITDA and PAT.

## Questions a deep-dive must answer
Out of mandate (governance plus cyclicality).
