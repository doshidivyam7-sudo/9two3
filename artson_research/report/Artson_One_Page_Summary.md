## BUSINESS IN ONE LINE
Specialist EPC contractor + pressure vessel manufacturer for Indian oil, gas, airports, and industrial clients. 75%-owned by Tata Projects Limited (unlisted Tata Sons subsidiary).

## THE KEY QUESTION
> Is Artson's ₹801 Crore market cap justified?
>
> **No.** On every fundamental metric (P/B, EV/EBITDA, ROCE, cash generation), Artson trades at 15–40x the appropriate multiple for its quality of business. The valuation is driven entirely by Tata brand speculation and retail momentum — not fundamentals.
>
> The company must first achieve **3 consecutive years** of positive EBITDA above 8%, positive net worth, and positive free cash flow before any investor should pay above ₹50/share.
