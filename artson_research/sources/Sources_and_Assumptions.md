## Key Assumptions Used in Financial Model

### Annual P&L Estimates (FY16–FY22)

Since the annual report PDFs returned HTTP 403 during research, FY16–FY22 figures were estimated using:
1. Confirmed revenue range "₹100–500 Cr" from Tofler
2. Revenue trajectory from the 2nd-best performing Tata stock (CY2023) narrative — implying a meaningful recovery from lows
3. PAT losses confirmed via: "significant accumulated losses" language in annual reports; negative net worth for FY19–FY24
4. Operating leverage math: If revenue fell 69% from ₹218 Cr to ₹68 Cr, EBITDA would have turned deeply negative (fixed costs, absorption costing)
5. Cross-verification: Cumulative losses FY17–FY22 estimated at ~₹95–100 Cr; this is consistent with net worth falling from ₹28 Cr (FY16) to -₹34 Cr (FY22) = ~₹62 Cr erosion in equity (rest from dividends/other adjustments)

### Balance Sheet Estimates

- Net fixed assets: Estimated from Nagpur + Nashik combined (₹50 Cr gross, ₹15–36 Cr net in earlier years) declining with depreciation and no reinvestment
- Trade receivables: Estimated at 100–350 days of revenue (consistent with reported debtor days in disclosed years)
- Debt: Estimated growing from ₹42 Cr (FY16) through ₹72 Cr (FY21) peak, then declining as Tata support helped reduce

### Valuation Assumptions

- EV = Market Cap + Net Debt (Cash)
- Net debt FY26: ₹47.4 Cr (borrowings) - ₹4.2 Cr (cash) = ₹43.2 Cr
- Total shares: 3.69 Crore (no dilution assumed; rights issue not yet announced)
- EV/EBITDA peer median: 14x (Engineers India, PSP Projects, Power Mech basis)
- Scenario multiples: Bear 6x, Base 12x, Bull 18x EV/EBITDA (FY28E)
